# Graphical abstract provenance

The artwork was composed deterministically from original vector shapes and text. No generative image model, third-party icons, photographs, or copyrighted visual assets were used.

The visual claims and design-control language were derived from the revised JCE manuscript. The quantitative magnitudes in the manuscript are model-specific; this abstract emphasizes the general methodological structure.

JCE's current guide states that AI-assisted graphical-abstract artwork is not permitted. The authors should therefore treat this file as a precise design draft and determine whether manual reconstruction is required before submission.
