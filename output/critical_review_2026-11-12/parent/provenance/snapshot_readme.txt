The snapshot manifest records the environment used for the review diagnostics. The primary run manifest records a different analysis environment. Neither record establishes the original cohort-generation environment. The exact historical environment should not be filled in from the current environment.

Microsoft Word remains authoritative for citation refresh, visual checks and final saves. The two files ending in _critical_review.docx are the Word-saved review copies. Do not replace them with newly generated drafts without repeating the Word checks.
