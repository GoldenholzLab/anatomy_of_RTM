Reporting-guideline explanation

The anatomy of regression to the mean in simulated epilepsy trials

This is a descriptive computational simulation using synthetic seizure
histories. It includes no human participants, observational patient sample,
randomised treatment comparison, diagnostic-accuracy validation or systematic
review. The corresponding clinical reporting checklists do not match this
study design, so no completed clinical checklist is supplied.

The manuscript reports the simulation cohort and generating process in
Section 2.1, selection and trial-window rules in Section 2.2, condition
transforms in Section 2.3, and outcome definitions, denominators and Monte
Carlo intervals in Section 2.4. Sections 3.1 to 3.4 and supplementary Tables
S1 to S3 give the results. Section 4.4 describes model and design limitations.
The revised analysis files include the protocol, source and input/output
hashes, deterministic seeds, numerical environment and reproduction commands.
Public release of the revised files remains pending.
